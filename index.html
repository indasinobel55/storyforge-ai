/ SYSTEM CAPABILITIES

Built like a machine, not a mockup.

Every stage of the studio is a real, verified component — from the planner to the final checksum. Nothing decorative, nothing fake.

01
PLANNER

Script to scene plan

A deterministic planner turns your story into a consistent shot list — characters, prompts, camera, lighting and continuity notes for every 8-second scene.

02
CONTINUITY

Frame-true continuity

The last frame of every scene is analyzed by a vision model. A continuation passage is written and injected into the next prompt, so scenes flow like one film.

03
EXECUTION

Durable, resumable jobs

Planning, generation, download, assembly and validation run as leased jobs. A crashed worker resumes from the last checkpoint instead of starting over.

04
VALIDATION

Real validation

Every clip and the final cut are probed server-side for decodability, duration and resolution before a project is ever marked complete.
