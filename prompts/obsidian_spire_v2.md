### CHARACTER CONTINUITY

**THE SHARD:** A small mysterious knight with a white porcelain mask-like face, a single vertical crack emitting rhythmic pale-blue light, a tattered charcoal cloak with frayed moth-wing-like edges, dark boots, and a slender needle-sword held at its side. No visible mouth. Same size, same cloak, same mask crack in every scene.

**THE OBSIDIAN SPIRE:** An enormous underground cathedral of polished black volcanic stone with inward-leaning pillars, hanging chains, and ancient sightless carved eyes in the walls. Always vast, hostile, near-black.
**THE MIRROR:** An ancient artifact with a rippling viscous liquid-silver surface on a plinth of weeping basalt, centre of the cathedral. It never behaves like ordinary glass.
